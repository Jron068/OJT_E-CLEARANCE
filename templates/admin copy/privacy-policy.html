Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The City College of Calamba â€“ Department of Computing and Informatics is committed to protecting the privacy and security of all personal information collected through the OJT Management System. This policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your data in accordance with applicable data privacy laws.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following personal information when you use this system:

  • Full name, student ID or employee number
  • Institutional email address
  • Department and academic program
  • OJT-related records including time logs, evaluations, and submissions
  • System usage logs for audit and security purposes

2. How We Use Your Information

Collected information is used exclusively for:

  • Managing and monitoring On-the-Job Training activities
  • Communicating with students, facilitators, and partner companies
  • Generating reports for academic and administrative purposes
  • Ensuring the security and integrity of the system

3. Data Storage and Security

All personal data is stored on secure institutional servers with access controls. We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect against unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction of your information.

Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing algorithms and are never stored in plain text. Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • OJT partner companies and host training establishments, as required by the program
  • Government agencies when required by law
  • Internal departments for administrative coordination

5. Data Retention

Personal data is retained for the duration required by institutional policies and applicable regulations. OJT records are typically retained for a minimum of five (5) years from the date of completion.

6. Your Rights

Under applicable data privacy laws, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal information held by the institution
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Object to processing of your personal data
  • File a complaint with the Data Privacy Officer

7. Cookies and Session Data

This system uses session cookies solely to maintain your authenticated session. No third-party tracking cookies are used.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any significant changes will be communicated through the system. Continued use of the system constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For privacy-related concerns, please contact the Data Privacy Officer of the City College of Calamba, Department of Computing and Informatics.
