Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

The City College of Calamba â€“ Department of Computing and Informatics ("the College") is committed to protecting the personal information of our students, faculty, and staff. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect personal data within the OJT Facilitator Management System in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) of the Philippines.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information necessary for the administration of the On-the-Job Training (OJT) program. This includes student identification details, academic program information, company placement data, submitted OJT documents, electronic signatures, and contact information of both students and facilitators.

3. How We Use Your Information

Collected information is used solely for the purpose of managing OJT requirements, verifying and processing student-submitted documents, facilitating communication between students and their assigned facilitators, generating reports for the Department, and maintaining academic records relevant to OJT compliance.

4. Data Storage and Security

All personal data is stored securely and access is restricted to authorized personnel only. The College implements appropriate technical and organizational measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of personal data. Electronic signatures are encrypted and stored in compliance with applicable laws.

5. Data Sharing

Personal information collected within this system will not be shared with third parties outside the College without the explicit consent of the data subject, except when required by law or when necessary for the administration of OJT-related requirements with partner companies and establishments, subject to appropriate data sharing agreements.

6. Data Subject Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to be informed of how your personal data is used, to access your personal data, to correct inaccurate personal data, to object to the processing of your personal data, and to lodge complaints with the National Privacy Commission. To exercise these rights, please contact the College's Data Protection Officer.

7. Retention of Data

Personal data collected through this system will be retained for the duration required by academic and legal requirements. Data that is no longer needed for its original purpose will be securely disposed of in accordance with established institutional records management policies.

8. Cookies and Session Data

This system may use session data and local storage to maintain your login state and preferences. No personal data is shared with external analytics or advertising services.

9. Amendments

The College reserves the right to amend this Privacy Policy at any time. Users will be notified of significant changes through the system's notification feature. Continued use of the system after amendments constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

For questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the Department of Computing and Informatics, City College of Calamba, or reach out to the designated Data Protection Officer through the official college communication channels.
