2. Information collected

Depending on portal features, information may include name, student ID, email, contact details, academic information, OJT company details, uploaded documents, and optional profile photo.

3. Use of information

  • Monitor OJT progress
  • Communicate OJT announcements
  • Review submitted forms
  • Prepare official OJT reports

4. Portal storage

Account, profile, OJT, notification, and document metadata are stored in the configured MySQL database. Uploaded documents and profile images are stored in the application upload directory on the server and are available only through authenticated portal routes.

5. Data sharing and security

Student submissions are available to authorized facilitators and administrators for review. Production deployment should use HTTPS, protected database credentials, restricted file-system access, retention rules, backups, and an institution-approved privacy notice.

6. Your rights

For questions or requests concerning personal data, contact the Department of Computing and Informatics, City College of Calamba, Calamba City, Laguna.
